Smoking and Mental Distress in NYC ZIP Codes

The data on the regression table shows that there is a positive correlation between smoking and mental distress with the r value equaling +0.77.

For every one percent of smoking there is a 0.49% increase in mental distress.

The p value was 0.000 indicating that the relationship is statistically significant.

This data indicates that higher smoking rates are associated with higher levels of mental distress among people within the study.
