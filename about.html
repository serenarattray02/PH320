About Me

Public Health Portfolio · Serena Rattray

I am a public health student with a strong interest in the intersection of health, research, and technology. I chose public health because of how broad and diverse the field is. It provides a way to improve health outcomes on a large scale while addressing real community challenges.

One area I care deeply about is disease prevention. I believe in the idea that prevention is better than a cure, a lesson that has influenced my perspective throughout my life and academic journey.

My career goal is to pursue work in epidemiology or public health data analysis. I am especially interested in using research and data to identify patterns, evaluate evidence, and support informed public health decisions.

Professional direction

Connecting evidence with action.

My academic interests include disease prevention, population health, and the responsible use of information. I want to continue developing the analytical and communication skills needed to translate research into clear, useful insights for communities and public health professionals.
