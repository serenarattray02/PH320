About Me

I am a Public Health student with a strong interest in the intersection of health and technology. I chose public health because of how broad and diverse the field is. It provides a way to improve health outcomes on a large scale while addressing real community challenges.

One area I care deeply about is disease prevention. I believe in the idea that prevention is better than a cure, a lesson that has influenced my perspective throughout my life and academic journey.

My career goal is to become either an Epidemiologist or a Public Health Data Analyst. I am especially interested in using data, research, and technology to support evidence-based public health solutions.
